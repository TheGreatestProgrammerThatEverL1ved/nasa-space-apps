# Scenario expansion — explicit teaching policy

The user's post-M4 authorization permits new scenarios. It does **not** revise the [audited wildfire rules](game-rules-technical.md). Historical wildfire-only phase restrictions remain provenance, not a prohibition on this newly authorized work. Flood and methane remain deferred.

## One evaluator, scenario-owned science

Mission definitions supply bilingual briefs, goals, available catalog, initial/passing configurations, references and evaluation context. Shared mass, cost, power, pointing, tracking, link, effective-service queue, storage, schedule and lifetime checks remain in one deterministic evaluator. New science policies cannot rescue a failed engineering gate. A context is bound to its scenario; missing/malformed policies or mismatched requests cannot pass.

## Urban heat: Read the City

**Purpose:** scheduled, city-scale thermal-pattern screening for an urban planning team. Contrast broad thermal patterns, not individual streets. This is not a calibrated land-surface-temperature retrieval, air-temperature measurement, public-health risk product, heatwave alert service or operational coverage promise.

The same provisional CIRC-like LWIR camera, 500 km orbit class, tracking assumptions, 3.1850496 GB/day fixed frame plan, 40% rejection, one funded year, $3M budget and 36-month schedule are reused. Urban has its **own synthetic nominal 100 km daylit city-strip target fixture**, assumed schedulable at a maximum 24 h geometric gap; the audited wildfire 90 km strip is not silently relabelled. No new spacecraft or payload performance is inferred.

### Reproducible teaching designs

- **Starting failure:** WF-B hardware with S-band and the ground network. Effective capacity **1.890 GB/day <3.1850496 GB/day**; the growing queue fails. Even the optimistic useful-return ceiling **1.134 GB/day <1.5 GB/day** proves insufficiency. That ceiling is explicitly labelled, not represented as established achieved delivery; latency and achieved useful delivery remain unknown while the queue is unstable. Repair: select X-band and recheck cost, power and all gates.
- **Passing alternative:** WF-B hardware with X-band and a single station. Capacity **12.6 GB/day**, useful products **1.91102976 GB/day**, effective scale **170.334026 m**, footprint **104.231570 km**, fixture latency **7.1 h**. Urban can accept that latency; wildfire cannot.
- The single-station setup plus annual cost is **$95,000** versus network **$320,000**; after reserve this saves **$258,750**. The difference is a game allocation, not a vendor quote.

All passes remain **MODEL_PASS_PROVISIONAL**. Physical observation-to-product age remains unverified because the queue smooths image arrivals. Finite latency is shown only under accepted stable service assumptions.
