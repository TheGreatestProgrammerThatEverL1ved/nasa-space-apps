## 1. Empty builds and consequential parts

### 1A. Fresh drafts

1. New Sandbox and Campaign drafts for all four missions start at **0/8 selected**. All eight slots (bus, payload, solar array, battery, radio, ground, orbit, launch service) require explicit selection, including singleton choices. Revisiting a draft can restore only what the player actually selected. Key drafts by mode and mission; provide a visible Clear Build action and per-slot removal. Changing mode, mission, locale or screen must not fill slots, overwrite unrelated builds, or turn previewed equipment into installed equipment. Remove hidden defaults and first-item substitutions, including after refunds or context changes. Where there is an existing explicit draft persistence format, preserve identifiable user choices and handle older ambiguous preset state without silently presenting it as the player's build. Keep existing campaign saves and credits.
2. Use a partial draft representation at the UI boundary. Launch and complete evaluation require eight valid IDs in the current context. An incomplete craft displays empty slots and only installed hardware, including in fallback. Show 0–8 progress and a clear next action; unavailable telemetry is labeled as such. You may show known costs from selected items with a label stating it is a subtotal. Incomplete, incompatible, unowned or stale configurations must never produce a launch result or reward through a shortcut. Engineering reached via the player flow obeys the same rules. Audited reference builds can remain available to tests and clearly labeled internal tooling.
3. The first-run and replayable academy also start with an empty isolated build. Guide intentional choices with concise highlights or short steps; selecting Continue alone must not equip a component. Retain the 7.1 h single-station → 3.1 h network lesson in the audited training context. Tutorial activity must not modify the player's drafts or award campaign credits.

### 1B. Hardware scope and balance

- Add **at least 12 new unique selectable IDs**, **at least two each** in these six categories: bus, payload, solar, battery, radio and ground. Count IDs once across the whole game. Ship **at least 35 total**. Leave orbit and launch service as honest singleton choices if additional supported options would require speculative mechanics. Each addition must have complete evaluator inputs, truthful fictional/provisional provenance where applicable, EN/UA labels, a short tradeoff, and a real relationship to at least one mission choice. Provide a discernible visual for onboard hardware when installed or inspected, including the no-WebGL fallback. Ground is a service preview. Reuse procedural models and differentiate physical form or placement where appropriate. Names, recolors and abstract stat boosts alone do not count. At least one new payload expands an existing thermal, visible, or relay path in a material way; ensure the new payload set supports at least two of those mission families.
- Every new ID must be selectable and exercised by an evaluator test in its supported context. At least **8 of the 12 required new IDs** must appear in verified passing loadouts across the four missions. For any remaining ID explain a credible niche or tradeoff in the catalog matrix, and provide an evaluator example of the specific metric it improves. Check new stations against relay timing evidence where supported; exclude unsupported combinations transparently. Keep the catalog and contexts modular. Reuse evaluator fields and physics. Clearly mark synthetic engineering values and analytical contact latencies.
- Define explicitly revisioned player balance contexts where needed, including mission-specific engineering USD caps. Preserve the audited contexts, including WF-A/B/C, all 15 boundaries, 7.1 → 3.1 h, link/queue/storage semantics, scenario roles and immutable results. USD funds the design, credits fund Research. Do not conflate either with merit.
- Verify for **each** of the four missions: (a) at least **two passing loadouts** affordable at the playable mission budget, differing in at least **two subsystem categories**, with an evaluator-backed Pareto tradeoff (one stronger metric or lower cost versus another metric); (b) at least **one complete technically valid build that fails solely on `ENG-BUDGET`** in that mission and can be repaired by changing **one or two explicit selections**; (c) at least **one different under-budget non-budget failure** with a comprehensible remedy; (d) a witness that every contract is beatable using legitimately reachable campaign inventory when it becomes available, with no forced dead purchases. Use real selectable options in the corresponding mode; the budget-only witness may use later earned upgrades, but must be reproducible in a normal save. Prefer loadouts whose passing costs are close enough to the cap that the choice matters. Report counts for the new set and all example build IDs, totals, limits, gate outcomes and fixes. A low raw pass percentage currently coexists with helpful presets and weak budget pressure, so do not use that percentage as the sole success criterion.
- Validate solvency across reachable progression states or use an equivalently strong documented invariant with automated witnesses. Preserve historical v1/v2/v3 entitlements, assigned rewards, actual paid bases, balances, refund safety, one-time rewards, free retries and no refund/rebuy profit. A new upgrade need not become a new mandatory contract prerequisite. Keep intentional acquisition choices, including at least two meaningful early purchase routes with different tradeoffs when using a new profile.

## 3. Research as a place players can find

1. Add a first-level **Research** route reachable in **one deliberate selection from the main menu** and directly from Campaign navigation. In Sandbox use a clear read-only entry with a deliberate Campaign action if purchasing requires Campaign; entering Research must not switch modes silently. Keep Back predictable and preserve drafts/scroll where useful.
2. Show a branching map with connected compact icon buttons. Ship **at least 12 total nodes and eight actually purchasable nodes**, adding four real upgrades in **at least three branches**. The existing four owned foundations do not count toward purchases. Do not replace existing owned parts or rename their persisted IDs. Each purchase unlocks an actual selectable catalog item, whose selection in Customize is separate. Use icons reflecting the equipment, compact branch labels and sufficient spacing; keep the DOM order useful for keyboard/screen readers. Preserve branch relationships on narrow screens with a readable stacked map.
3. Required outlines are **yellow = can buy now**, **red = cannot buy** (including prerequisite or insufficient funds), **green = owned** (including foundations). Add shape/text or glyph status cues independent of color. Hover or focus previews **name, price, effect and prerequisite or lock reason** in one inspector. A tap/focus on touch must expose the same information, and the price must be visible before a paid activation. For mouse and keyboard, click or Enter/Space on an available node buys once. On touch use a first-tap preview then re-tap of the same node to purchase when price is visible. A locked/owned press must not debit or trigger purchase. Keep clickable nodes inspectable despite locked/owned state. Refund controls belong in the inspector if permitted by existing rules; refund clears any now-unavailable equipped slot with an explanation instead of substituting a part. Test reload and historical ledger samples.
